# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Principal investigators, radiology research groups and program coordinators deciding whether
to talk to Vanio Antunes about a research position; clinicians and researchers who arrive
through Google Scholar, LinkedIn or X; and systematic reviewers looking for his free tools and
R packages. Many open the site on a phone from a social link, so the phone view matters as much
as the desktop view.

## Product Purpose

vanioantunes.com is the personal site of Vanio Antunes, a final-year medical student who works
on meta-analysis methods and medical software and wants to move into radiology and 3D
reconstruction. The home page offers three paths: Who am I (trajectory, publications,
teaching, contact), Meta-analysis tools (ssHelper and tools in progress) and R packages
(easyDTA, nmaplots, meta3l). Success means a visitor understands who he is and what he builds
within seconds, and reaches the tool or repository they came for in one click.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm, personal. A researcher who writes the code. Quiet visuals, precise wording,
real numbers from real sources (Google Scholar), and a clear human presence through his photo
and trajectory.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, fake metrics, "Get started" clutter.
- Dated academic portal sites: cramped tiny text, grey tables, old styling.
- Oversized mobile layouts where one card fills the screen and the visitor has to scroll a lot
  to see anything.

## Design Principles

1. Three clear paths from the home page; every page shares the same pinned top bar.
2. Show real work: repository screenshots, the working tool, citation counts from Scholar.
3. Compact on phones: smaller type and spacing so a screen shows meaningful content.
4. Precision over flourish: exact numbers, plain sentences, no decoration for its own sake.
5. Static and calm: no scroll-jacking or reveal animations on content pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: color contrast, 24px minimum targets, full keyboard use with visible focus,
screen-reader names for icon links. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
