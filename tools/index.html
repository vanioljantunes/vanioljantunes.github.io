Meta-analysis tools

Free tools for systematic reviews. They run in the browser.

  • ssHelper

    Build and test PubMed search strategies: arms, result counts, and known-study checks.

    Open ssHelper

  • triageHelper beta

    Triage a Rayyan citation export against your eligibility criteria: each citation is scored and sorted into Critical, Likely, Unlikely or Structural. Under test, and free to use.

    Open triageHelper

  • Diagnostic calculator

    Build the confusion matrix from sensitivity, specificity, the number of people with the outcome and the sample size.

    Open calculator

  • Combine means and SDs

    Combine the sample size, mean and SD of two or more groups with Cochrane's formula.

    Open calculator

  • Median to mean

    Estimate the mean and SD from the median with the range and/or the interquartile range.

    Open calculator
